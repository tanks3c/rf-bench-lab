What this site leaves out

  • No home address or home coordinates. The tower map's distance-from-center field was removed because it could be used to locate the center point.
  • No MQTT usernames, passwords, or broker credentials.
  • No Meshtastic or MeshCore node IDs.
  • No analytics, accounts, cookies, or third-party scripts. Map libraries ship with the site.

Tower and repeater entries come from public directories and records. Many are historical listings and do not prove a site is active. Checklist progress and quiz answers stay in your browser.

Using it offline

Open any page once while online. The browser then saves every hub page, the RF Bench Lab, and the map files. To keep it on your phone, use your browser's Add to Home screen or Install option.

Still needs a connection: map background tiles, the live county GIS pull, live APRS data, and external links.
