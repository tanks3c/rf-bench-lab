Coverage and limitations

Countywide, but not every transmitter: Manatee County says its tower layer represents known cell towers and large radio/TV towers; smaller antennae may be missing. Its cellular layer covers known cell sites, with one representative point per site. A location is not proof of an active LMR system or specific transmit frequency. Overlapping features from the two layers are kept as separate source records; the count is records, not necessarily distinct towers.

Each row includes original county fields and the source URL. Records can contain older names or ownership information. This app never changes county GIS data.
